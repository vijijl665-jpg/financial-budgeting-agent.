F. Business agent explanation

The listed expenses are summed once. Available funds before savings are income minus expenses; the savings rate is the goal divided by income. Savings are kept separate from expenses, avoiding double-counting. Because the post-savings balance is positive, it is assigned to contingency rather than treated as additional spending. Expense classifications use the stated assumptions above.

G. Actionable recommendations

Small habits that keep the plan realistic.

  1. Fund savings early: set aside the fictional ₹10,000 target after income arrives, while keeping required bills covered.
  2. Protect the ₹6,000 buffer: leave it available for irregular or unexpected costs; avoid counting it as routine spending.
  3. Review flexible costs monthly: check the ₹1,000 leisure and ₹1,000 incidental estimates against actual needs before cutting anything.
  4. Verify the assumptions: replace the illustrative expense breakdown with fictional, categorized amounts if doing another lab run.
